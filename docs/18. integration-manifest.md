  Technical-only report builder. It consumes the existing
  `TechnicalCartonDocument.getArtworkSurfaces()` contour/role data and current
  ArtworkModel state; it does not mutate either model and does not persist the
  report. Unknown semantic roles and malformed contours fail closed as
  `unknown` rather than using a label or a bounding-box assertion.
* Surface policy is exact-role based. RTE and STE exclude
  `BODY.SIDE_SEAM.GLUE_FLAP` as `glue-surface`; TT_SL123 additionally excludes
  its four `CLOSURE.BOTTOM.SNAP_LOCK.*` surfaces as `locking-surface`. Body
  front/back/sides, major/dust flaps and tuck tongues are printable. The
  classification is independent of localized labels.
* Coverage uses canonical LINE/ARC contours. ARC flattening remains confined to
  diagnostic validation (`π/96` maximum angular step; `1e-5 mm` contour
  join/radius tolerance), while coverage itself uses analytic circle/line
  intersections and exact ARC boundary/scanline checks; it does not treat a
  sampled chord as proof. `1e-6 mm` coverage tolerance is used for interval
  comparisons. Internal gaps and ARC bulge outside the artwork footprint are
  detected; if the union cannot prove coverage, the result is `unknown`.
  Footprints account for global position, crop, independent/proportional scale,
  90-degree rotations and flips. Hidden, finish and empty/invalid layers are
  excluded from coverage.
* Raster quality uses `ArtworkModel.getEffectiveDpi()`: `<300` is
  `dpi-below-recommended`, `>=300` is pass, vector sources (including PDF) are
  pass without a raster DPI warning, and unavailable dimensions are
  `dpi-unknown`. No new blocking `<150` rule was introduced.
* `ArtworkApp` exposes a cloned read-only `getTechnicalArtworkPreflight()` and
  adds the Technical quality summary. Quick `getExportWarnings()` and Quick
  geometry remain unchanged. The existing `technical-prepress-unavailable`
  and `technicalPrepressUnavailable` production-assist guards remain active.
  Preview, Render, PBD/schema/archive, provenance and flat export boundaries
  were not enabled or reworked by this slice.
* Missing or non-array `getArtworkSurfaces()` is a technical-carton-invalid
  unknown result, effective DPI is accepted only as a finite positive number,
  and surface reports do not expose unproven `artworkIds` contributor lists.

Fixture classification:

| Fixture | Excluded surfaces | Printable surfaces |
|---|---|---|
| RTE | exact glue flap role | body front/back/sides and normal closure flaps/tongues |
| STE | exact glue flap role | body front/back/sides and normal closure flaps/tongues |
| TT_SL123 | exact glue flap role plus four `SNAP_LOCK` roles | body panels and non-locking closure flaps/tongues |
